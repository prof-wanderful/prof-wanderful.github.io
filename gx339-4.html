GX 339-4: Broadband Spectral–Timing Study

Multi-mission X-ray analysis of the 2023–2024 outburst of the black hole X-ray binary GX 339-4

This page accompanies our broadband spectral–timing study of GX 339-4 during its 2023–2024 outburst. The selected epochs trace the source from an intermediate state close to the HIMS/SIMS boundary, through a clear soft-intermediate state with a narrow low-frequency QPO, and finally into a disk-dominated soft state.

Epoch 1: HIMS/SIMS boundary Epoch 2: SIMS with ~4.6 Hz LFQPO Epoch 3: Soft / thermal state

Project Overview

GX 339-4 is a recurrent black hole low-mass X-ray binary and a benchmark source for studying accretion-state evolution. In this work, we use coordinated observations from NICER, NuSTAR, AstroSat, INTEGRAL, and XMM-Newton to investigate how the thermal disk, Comptonized continuum, relativistic reflection, and fast variability evolve during the 2023–2024 outburst.

Epochs 1 and 2 have similar time-averaged broadband spectra, with disk temperatures near 0.76 keV and photon indices near 2.14–2.16. The key distinction is provided by timing: Epoch 2 shows a narrow LFQPO near 4.6 Hz, detected with NICER, NuSTAR, and AstroSat/LAXPC. Epoch 3 is cooler and more disk dominated, with substantially weaker reflection.

Analysis Highlights

  • Broadband spectral fitting was performed with a common model including disk emission, thermal Comptonization, relativistic reflection, interstellar absorption, empirical partial covering, and soft-band edge corrections.
  • Epoch 2 provides the widest spectral coverage, from approximately 0.6 to 200 keV, using NICER, NuSTAR, AstroSat/SXT, AstroSat/LAXPC, INTEGRAL/ISGRI, and INTEGRAL/SPI.
  • A narrow LFQPO near 4.6 Hz is detected during Epoch 2. Its rms increases from about 1–1.2% in the NICER 1–10 keV band to about 8% in the NuSTAR/LAXPC 3–50 keV band.
  • Epoch 3 shows a cooler disk, reduced Comptonized contribution, and weak reflection, consistent with a soft/thermal-state classification.

Spectral Model

The broadband spectra were fitted using the model:

constant × edge × pcfabs × TBabs × (thcomp × diskbb + relxillCp)

The diskbb component describes the multicolour thermal disk, thcomp accounts for Comptonization of disk photons, and relxillCp models the relativistic reflection spectrum. The reflection fraction was fixed to −1 so that relxillCp contributes only the reflected component. The black-hole spin was fixed at a = 0.985, the electron temperature at 150 keV, the emissivity indices at q1 = q2 = 3, and the outer disk radius at 400 rg. The pcfabs and edge terms are used as empirical corrections for residual soft-band curvature and calibration features.
